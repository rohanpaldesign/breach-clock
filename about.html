About

Cybercrime, counted.

Attacks in flight, money lost, the biggest breaches and how they happen. Every number links to its source.

What’s here

Live shows DDoS attacks moving between countries at the real average pace. Since you arrived counts money lost and data exposed while you read. On the record maps attacks researchers traced to a country. Scale draws the biggest breaches one dot per million records, and How it happens walks through five common attacks.

How to read the numbers

Counters spread a published yearly total evenly over time, so they are averages, not live feeds. Each figure is marked as measured, second-hand, the attackers’ own claim or an estimate, and every one links to the report it came from on the Sources page.
