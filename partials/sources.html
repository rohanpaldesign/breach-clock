How the counters work

Each counter takes a published total for a period and spreads it evenly: total ÷ seconds in the period. Real crime arrives in bursts, so read them as averages, not live feeds. Nothing on this page is tracked live.

What the numbers don’t cover

FBI figures are only what victims reported, mostly in the US. Breach notices count notices, not unique people. Ransom totals are payments traceable on blockchains. Attackers inflate their own numbers, so claims are marked.
