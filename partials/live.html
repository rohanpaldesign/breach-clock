How the live view works

Cloudflare blocked 23.2 million DDoS attacks in the first half of 2026, about 5,343 an hour. Each route gets a share of that: attacks a minute × source country’s share × target country’s share, using the shares Cloudflare Radar reported for the 7 days to 16 November 2026. Attacks are then drawn at random at that average rate, so the timing is simulated but the pace and directions are real.

What it doesn’t tell you

The source country is where the attack traffic came from, usually hijacked computers and rented servers, not where the attacker lives. It covers DDoS attacks Cloudflare saw, only the top 20 countries on each side (74% of sources), and assumes a country’s attacks spread across targets in proportion to how often each is hit. For attacks pinned on a country, see On the record.
